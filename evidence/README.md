# Evidence

The knowledge base every skill reads from.
One file per practical topic, organized by the priority pyramids popularized by Eric Helms (The Muscle and Strength Pyramids): what matters most comes first.

## Principles

- **What works, as simple as possible, for you.**
- **Evidence over common sense.** A rule with no good source does not get in, however popular it is.
- **Minimum effective dose over optimal on paper.** The default is the simplest option that delivers most of the result. Going further is opt-in.
- **Adherence first.** A plan that is 90% optimal and followed beats a perfect plan that is abandoned.
- **Profile filters before science.** Age, injuries, equipment, time and food preferences remove options before any optimization.

## Source hierarchy

1. Position stands and consensus statements (ISSN, ACSM).
2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
3. Evidence-based reviewers (Helms, Stronger by Science, Nippard) as a map to find and cross-check the literature. Cite the paper, credit the reviewer.
4. Single studies only when nothing above exists, flagged as weak.

Every DOI is checked against Crossref or OpenAlex, and every number against the abstract or full text.

## Levels

Same evidence, different resolution.

| | pupil | grinder | maxxer |
|---|---|---|---|
| who | new to the gym, knows very little | has trained and dieted before, not confident yet | advanced, wants to keep progressing |
| detail | simplest rules, no tracking beyond the basics | key numbers (sets, protein, calories) | full numbers, weekly adjustments |
