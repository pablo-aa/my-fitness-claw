NOTEThis plan is a starting point made by AI. It does not replace a dietitian, a coach or a doctor. If you have an illness, an injury or frequent pain, talk to one of them before you start.

4 workouts, 1,600 kcal and 8k steps a day.

Training

Stop each set when you could do only 1 or 2 more reps.

Monday · Upper A

  • Bench press 3 × 5-8
  • Chest-supported row 3 × 6-10
  • Shoulder press 3 × 8-12
  • Lat pulldown 3 × 8-12
  • Lateral raise 3 × 12-20
  • Rope pushdown 2 × 10-15
  • Barbell curl 2 × 10-15

Tuesday · Lower A

  • Romanian deadlift 3 × 6-10
  • Leg press 3 × 10-15
  • Lying leg curl 3 × 10-15
  • Hip thrust 3 × 8-12
  • Standing calf raise 3 × 10-15
  • Cable crunch 2 × 10-15

Thursday · Upper B

  • Incline press 3 × 8-12
  • Pull-up 3 × 6-10
  • Seated row 3 × 10-12
  • Peck deck 2 × 12-15
  • Lateral raise 3 × 12-20
  • Overhead extension 2 × 10-15
  • Hammer curl 2 × 10-15

Friday · Lower B

  • Heavy hip thrust 3 × 6-10
  • Seated leg curl 3 × 10-15
  • Leg press, feet high 3 × 10-15
  • Leg extension 2 × 12-15
  • Hip abduction 2 × 12-20
  • Seated calf raise 3 × 10-15

When to add weight

  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set? Add weight next time.
  • First week: go a little lighter than usual and focus on form.
  • On a diet, keeping your weights is already a win.

Food

1,600 kcal a day, with 130 g of protein.

Breakfast2 slices of bread, 2 eggs and 1 slice of cheese400 kcal
LunchEating out: half a plate of salad, a palm of protein, a fist of carbs600 kcal
Snack1 protein yogurt and 1 fruit200 kcal
Dinner100 g of rice, 150 g of chicken or fish and vegetables550 kcal

Swaps

  • 150 g chicken = 130 g lean beef = 170 g fish
  • 100 g rice = 90 g pasta = 170 g potato = 1 bread roll
  • 100 g beans = 100 g lentils
  • 1 scoop whey = 1 protein yogurt = 100 g chicken
  • 1 slice of cheese = 1 tbsp cream cheese

Weigh everything cooked.

Free meal

Once a week, instead of dinner. A burger with a diet soda fits easily.

Lower back

  • Brace before every lift and keep the bar close to the body.
  • If it aches, swap barbell rows for chest-supported rows and Romanian deadlifts for hip thrusts.

Cardio

Cycling 2 times a week, 30 to 40 minutes at a pace where you can still talk.

Every week

Step 1 · Weigh

Every morning, after the bathroom. The weekly average counts, not the daily number.

Step 2 · Compare

The average should drop about 0.25 kg a week, down to 61 by Jun 1.

Step 3 · Adjust

Dropped less than 0.2 kg? Cut a little rice or add a cycling session. The first week does not count.
