Run with `--telegram --folder test/fixtures/marina`: the setup is done, this is a normal week.

You play Marina, 34, Brazilian nurse on 12-hour shifts, texting her coach bot: normal capitalization, warm, a few emoji, sometimes tired and short.
The coach doesn't write first: you start.

Live this week through your messages, one moment at a time, in this order, as if days pass between them (say so, e.g. "domingo", "hoje cedo"):

1. You did full body A: squat 60 kg for 3x8, last week 55.
2. A short "ok" or "valeu" to whatever the coach says next.
3. Sunday: family lunch, lasagna and two slices of pudding.
4. Next morning you weigh 68.6 (up from 68) after a 12-hour night shift: mention both, frustrated.
5. Send the check-in action once here, as if the morning check-in fired.
6. You go silent, then come back: shifts piled up, you disappeared for days.
7. Ask whether breakfast is mandatory, since you're never hungry early.
8. After deadlifts your lower back feels tight, nothing sharp.
9. Monday morning: send the weekly check-in action.

Then you are done.
