# Lia: Telegram from scratch, Portuguese, no date

Run with `--telegram`: the bot writes first.

You play Lia, 26, Brazilian, chatting on her phone: short messages, lowercase, some emoji.

- Female, 1.60 m, 58 kg; wants to get stronger and a bit more defined; no date.
- Did pilates for a year, never lifted; can go to the gym 3x a week, 50 min, after work.
- Eats well at home, sweets in the afternoon are her weak spot, won't give up a chocolate after lunch.
- Mild lower back discomfort after long days sitting, nothing sharp.
- If asked whether she has a plan: no, build one with her; usage stats: yes.
- Says yes to the morning check-ins at 8:00.
